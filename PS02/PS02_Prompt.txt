- Steady-state margin = waves and managed support, excluding Discovery.
- Discovery may run below 18% if blended and steady-state margins stay at or
  above 18%.
- Price floor = total delivery cost / 0.82.
- Delivery cost = people + tools/environments + transition + 5% contingency
  on people cost.
- Rates:
  - 160 hours per FTE-month.
  - Cost per FTE-month: onshore USD 12,000; offshore USD 1,200.
  - Bill per FTE-month: onshore USD 13,400; offshore USD 4,750.
  - Mix: 25% onshore / 75% offshore.
- Blended cost rate = USD 3,900. Blended bill rate = USD 6,912.50.
- Rate margin = 43.6%.
- Price = FTE-months x USD 6,912.50.
- People cost = FTE-months x USD 3,900.
