# Deliverable Quality Checklist — Instructions

> **Purpose:** Use this checklist to review your deliverable before you submit it. It works as a self-review by the team, a peer review by another team, or a prompt for an AI assistant.
> **Target:** A world-class deliverable that a CxO can read, trust and act on.

---

## How to Use

1. **Assign a reviewer.** Someone who did not write the deliverable gives the most honest review.
2. **Go through all 8 sections in order.** Mark each item ✅ **Yes**, ⚠️ **Partly** or ❌ **No**.
3. **Score it.** Yes = 2, Partly = 1, No = 0. Add up the score for each section.
4. **Fix every ❌ first,** starting with Sections 1–3, then improve the ⚠️ items.
5. **Run the final test** at the end before you submit.

---

## Section 1 — Executive Summary (CxO Impact)

- [ ] The executive summary comes first and fits on **one page**.
- [ ] It states the **decision or recommendation** in one sentence.
- [ ] It shows **3 headline numbers** for the impact on cost, revenue, risk, time or quality.
- [ ] It names the **top risk** and how you will reduce it.
- [ ] It gives a clear **ask**: who must approve what, and by when.
- [ ] A CxO could act after reading **only this page**.

## Section 2 — Storyline & Structure

- [ ] The **answer comes first**, then the supporting evidence, then the detail.
- [ ] Every heading is a **message**, not a topic (e.g. "Release freeze cuts incidents by 60%", not "Analysis").
- [ ] Reading **only the headings** tells the full story.
- [ ] The sections follow a logical order: problem, then options, then recommendation, then plan.
- [ ] There is no repetition and no content that doesn't support the story.

## Section 3 — Problem Understanding

- [ ] The problem is stated clearly in business terms.
- [ ] The root causes are identified, not just the symptoms.
- [ ] **Facts are separated from assumptions.**
- [ ] The stakeholders and their competing priorities are acknowledged.

## Section 4 — Evidence & Credibility

- [ ] Every number has a source or a clear basis.
- [ ] All assumptions are **labelled clearly**.
- [ ] Synthetic or sample data is marked **"Illustrative"**.
- [ ] The figures you were given are used **without changes**.
- [ ] There are no unsupported claims or exaggerated results.
- [ ] The calculations are checked and add up consistently across pages.

## Section 5 — Recommendation & Options

- [ ] At least **2 options** were considered.
- [ ] The recommended option is clearly justified.
- [ ] The **trade-offs** are stated openly (e.g. speed vs. quality, cost vs. risk).
- [ ] The benefits, costs, dependencies and risks are each covered.
- [ ] The recommendation is **realistic** and can be achieved with the resources available.

## Section 6 — Action Plan & Ownership

- [ ] The plan has phases (e.g. 30 / 60 / 90 days).
- [ ] Every action has an **owner** and a **date**.
- [ ] Success measures show the **baseline, the target and the review date**.
- [ ] The plan includes checkpoints and points where you decide to continue or stop.
- [ ] Risks have a response plan and an owner.

## Section 7 — Clarity & Visual Quality

- [ ] Each page or slide has **one key message**.
- [ ] Visuals (charts, tables, flows) are used in place of dense text.
- [ ] The text is short and free of jargon, and acronyms are explained.
- [ ] Fonts, colours and layout are consistent throughout.
- [ ] The font is readable, with no clutter and nothing overlapping.
- [ ] There are no spelling or grammar errors.
- [ ] Page numbers and the file name follow the agreed convention.

## Section 8 — AI Usage & Responsible Practice

- [ ] **Only approved AI tools** were used.
- [ ] Prompts are recorded, showing the first prompt, the refined prompt and how the output was checked.
- [ ] AI output was **reviewed and corrected by people**, not pasted in as it came.
- [ ] Every team member took part in using AI.
- [ ] **No confidential, customer, employee or proprietary data** is included.

---

## Scoring Guide

| Total Score | Rating | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **85–100%** | 🏆 World-class | Ready to submit |
| **70–84%** | ✅ Strong | Fix the ⚠️ items in Sections 1–4 |
| **50–69%** | ⚠️ Needs work | Rework the executive summary and storyline |
| **Below 50%** | ❌ Not ready | Restructure the deliverable |

> **Must-pass items (these are needed whatever the score):** a one-page executive summary, a clear recommendation, labelled assumptions, owners on actions, and no confidential data.

---

## Final 5-Question Test

1. Would a CxO **make a decision in 60 seconds** from page one?
2. Would a sceptical reviewer **trust every number**?
3. Is it obvious **who does what, by when**?
4. Could anything be **removed** without losing meaning? If so, remove it.
5. Would the team be **proud to present this** to the CEO?

**If any answer is "No", fix it before you submit.**

## Optional: AI Review Prompt

Copy this prompt into an approved AI tool, along with your deliverable:

```
Act as a CxO-level reviewer. Assess the attached deliverable against this
checklist (Sections 1–8). For each section, give a score (0–2 per item),
list the top 3 gaps, and suggest specific fixes. Finally, rewrite the
executive summary to be sharper and decision-focused in under 150 words.
```
