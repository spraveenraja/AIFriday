Apply five simple portfolio rules

  1. Rank by customer impact, date criticality, service/contract exposure, dependency readiness and effort fit.
  2. Reserve support, testing and release capacity; plan no person above 100%.
  3. Give the two constrained roles focused weekly blocks; record displaced work.
  4. Disposition each pending change with effort, schedule, cost and value before starting.
  5. Separate committed, at-risk and proposed dates; confirm changes with customers.
Stakeholders: customers need outcomes and credible dates; program leads need feasible scope; specialists need realistic load; support/quality need protected capacity; Finance needs margin; the Portfolio Sponsor arbitrates.

Evidence boundary: Program identities, actual dates, role names/hours, estimates, penalties and margin drivers were not supplied. No account-specific allocations or savings are invented. Any sample records must be labelled Illustrative.
